About VVCE

Vidyavardhaka College of Engineering (VVCE), Mysuru, is an autonomous institution known for academic excellence, innovation, and industry-oriented technical education. The institution actively supports research, student chapters, and global technical collaborations.

About IEEE

IEEE is the world's largest technical professional organization dedicated to advancing technology for humanity. It supports global collaboration in engineering, computing, and innovation through standards, conferences, and publications.

About IEEE Computational Intelligence Society

IEEE CIS focuses on artificial intelligence, neural networks, fuzzy systems, evolutionary computation, and machine learning. The society promotes research, competitions, and student innovation in computational intelligence technologies.
